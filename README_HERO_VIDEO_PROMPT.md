# Hero Video Prompt

Updated: 2026-08-29

This prompt pack is for generating a premium portfolio intro background video.

Recommended output:
- Duration: 10-12 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or 4K
- Frame rate: 24-30 fps
- No text
- No logos
- Seamless forward-moving camera shot
- Dark enough for white hero typography overlay

## Master Prompt

Create a 12-second cinematic portfolio intro as one continuous forward-moving camera journey.

Begin in a nearly black futuristic digital environment. At the center floats an elegant, highly advanced crystalline AI quantum core, a transparent cube built from microscopic circuits, luminous data pathways, holographic layers, and fine particles. Electric blue and subtle violet light flows through the structure. Thousands of tiny data particles and delicate neural-like connections surround the core. The camera slowly pushes toward the glowing center.

As the camera reaches the center, the internal data pathways begin rotating. The cube dissolves naturally into billions of luminous blue and violet particles. These particles form a massive spiraling digital vortex. The camera is pulled directly into the vortex. Streams of microscopic data, light trails, and particles curve around the camera. The motion accelerates dramatically as the digital world transforms into something cosmic.

At the center of the vortex appears an impossibly bright singularity. Everything compresses into a single white point of tension, then erupts into a spectacular Big Bang directly in front of the camera. A white-hot core explodes outward. Blue, violet, white, and subtle warm golden energy expands through deep black space. Millions of particles, plasma clouds, cosmic dust, and streaks of light rush past the camera. The camera continues flying forward through the explosion.

The explosion expands into deep space. Hot plasma cools into enormous cosmic clouds. Stars ignite throughout darkness. Nebula structures and distant star systems form naturally from the expanding particles. The camera keeps moving forward through the newly formed universe without stopping.

Cosmic material begins rotating around a brilliant gravitational center. A magnificent spiral galaxy forms. Billions of stars curve into vast spiral arms. The camera approaches the galaxy from above, then accelerates toward one spiral arm at enormous scale.

The camera dives into the galaxy and transitions into an exhilarating high-speed journey through deep space. Stars stretch into cinematic light trails. The camera passes asteroid fields, enormous celestial structures, and a massive planet while maintaining high forward velocity. End with the camera still moving into deep space so the final frame can transition naturally into a portfolio website hero.

Visual direction: premium cinematic science fiction, photorealistic, elegant, sophisticated, deep blacks, electric blue, controlled violet highlights, subtle warm stellar orange during the Big Bang, volumetric lighting, realistic particles, realistic cosmic dust, strong depth, massive scale, subtle lens bloom, smooth physically believable camera motion, high contrast, dark enough for white UI text to remain readable.

## Negative Prompt

Avoid:
- text
- numbers
- logos
- watermarks
- human characters
- human brain imagery
- robot faces
- humanoid AI
- cyberpunk city scenes
- spaceship cockpit
- large UI screens
- retro HUD graphics
- video-game interface look
- cartoon style
- cheap neon effects
- abrupt cuts
- slideshow transitions
- handheld camera shake
- excessive lens flare
- fully overexposed frames

## Model Notes

### Sora / Veo / Kling
- Use the Master Prompt first.
- Add the Timeline Prompt if the model supports structured shot guidance.
- Add the Negative Prompt in the model's "avoid" or negative field.

### For Portfolio Use

- Prepare a poster frame from the cleanest mid-scene frame.
